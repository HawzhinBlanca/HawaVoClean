# Release risk register

This is the operational risk view for the HawaVoClean 3.3 candidate. `Controlled` means the named
automated evidence exists; it does not mean zero risk. `Open` blocks a 10/10 release. `Pending final`
means a control passed earlier but must be repeated on the eventual release commit. Volatile proof
facts come from [the generated release status](docs/generated-release-status.md); task ownership and
acceptance criteria come from [the true-10 plan](docs/true-10-plan.md).

The guard is explicitly not ASR or a linguistic oracle. | Approve the locked protocol/source route; acquire licensed speaker-disjoint audio; dual-review every unit; adjudicate; pass zero content-integrity events with the locked statistical bound. ## Non-negotiable release rule

No open critical/high item above may be described as “mitigated enough” by implication. It closes only
through the named evidence or an explicit, recorded user risk decision where the project cannot own
the underlying control. A new source commit, Resolve build, Electron version, corpus digest, protocol
digest, or advisory result invalidates the corresponding acceptance and requires re-evaluation.
